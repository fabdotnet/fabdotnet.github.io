## Ideas Were Never the Problem

I have always had ideas for tools: small utilities that solve a problem I keep
running into, and that other people in the community would probably find
useful too. I have always wanted to share them.

The hard part was never the idea. It was everything after it.

## The Missing Step

Turning an idea into something worth publishing takes real development time,
and even more testing time. Shipping a buggy release was never an option: a
tool that breaks on someone else's machine does more harm than no tool at all.

Between work and everything else, I never had enough time to get through that
step. Most of my ideas stayed ideas, or ended up as a half-finished script on a
disk somewhere.

## What Changed

AI changed that. Development time has dropped so much that some days I wonder
whether finding the right idea now takes longer than building it. ^^

Projects that used to stall at the prototype stage now reach the point where
they can be packaged, documented and shared.

## So, To Be Clear

**AI is the main author of the tools, and of the articles, published on this
blog.**

The ideas, the needs behind them and the choices of what gets published are
mine. Most of the code and the writing is produced with AI.

If you find something odd or badly written, I apologize in advance, and I will
try to review more carefully. In the meantime, consider everything here as
**best effort**: best effort in tokens, yes, but best effort in brain power too. ^^

Feedback, issues and pull requests are always welcome.
